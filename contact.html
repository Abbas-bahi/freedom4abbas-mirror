Write to Abbas

Abbas reads his mail. If you write to him, please follow the facility's rules so your letter actually reaches him.

Send a letter

Abbas Javed Ahmed #1182708Greensville Correctional Center901 Corrections WayJarratt, VA 23870
  • General letters go to the facility address above — not to the mail distribution center.
  • All incoming mail is photocopied: Abbas receives a copy, not your original pages. Keep anything precious at home.
  • Keep letters to at most 3 sheets written on both sides, or 5 single-sided pages. Mail over the limit is returned to the sender.
  • Write your full return address on the envelope and use proper postage.

Message his tablet

You can also send Abbas electronic messages to his prison tablet through ConnectNetwork (ViaPath). It works like email, but through their system — messages are not free; they use purchased credits.

  1. Create a free account at ConnectNetwork.com or in the ConnectNetwork app, then verify it from the activation email they send you.
  2. Add Virginia Department of Corrections, then add Abbas as a contact: Abbas Javed Ahmed, state ID 1182708, Greensville Correctional Center. He may need to accept the contact on his tablet before messages flow.
  3. Buy messaging credits with a debit or credit card, then write from the Messaging tab — up to about 2,000 characters per message. In the app you can attach photos where the facility allows.
  4. If you can, attach a reply credit so Abbas can answer you at no cost to him.

Every message is screened by facility staff before it reaches his tablet, so delivery can take from a few hours to a couple of days — it is not instant. ConnectNetwork support: 1-877-650-4249 or 1-800-483-8314.

Send money (commissary)

Commissary money for Virginia state prisons goes through JPay — not through the facility. Never mail cash, checks, or money orders to the prison itself; they will be rejected.

Online (fastest)

Create an account at JPay.com or in the JPay app, choose Virginia, and enter Abbas Javed Ahmed, state ID 1182708. Pay by Visa or Mastercard. JPay charges a fee — roughly $2.95 for a small deposit up to $9.95 for a large one; the exact fee is shown before you pay. Money typically becomes available within two to four business days.

Send money on JPayVirginia DOC: sending money

By mail (no JPay fee)

Buy a money order payable to JPay, write “Abbas Javed Ahmed — 1182708” on the memo line, print and fill out the JPay deposit slip from JPay.com, and mail both to: JPay, P.O. Box 278170, Miramar, FL 33027. It is posted within about three business days after JPay receives it.

  • Virginia law puts 10% of every deposit into a mandatory savings account until it reaches $1,000, and any court fines or costs owed are deducted automatically.
  • State rules allow you to send money to one incarcerated person unless the department approves more.
  • Money for his tablet, messages, and phone calls is separate — that goes through ConnectNetwork (PIN Debit), not JPay. ConnectNetwork payment options
