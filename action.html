No elected official, clerk, or review body has been named. Leave the greeting blank until an appropriate public office is supplied. Do not use this letter to accuse or threaten anyone.

To: Conviction Review Unit
Office of the Commonwealth’s Attorney
Arlington County and the City of Falls Church
1425 North Courthouse Road
Arlington, VA 22201
Email: CWA_CRU@arlingtonva.us

Subject: Request for a careful review of the public record concerning Abbas Ahmed

Dear Conviction Review Unit,

I am writing respectfully to ask that your office review the conviction of Abbas Ahmed, to the extent that review fits your role. I am not asking you to prejudge him, and I am not presenting this message as proof of any fact.

Materials gathered for public education include a November 10, 2004 Division of Forensic Science certificate, lab number N04-7595, and 2005 defense letters about the interrogation and the medical report. Those letters are opinions. The certificate does not, by itself, establish innocence. Parts of the court record, including the sentencing orders and the appellate opinions, have not been posted on the advocacy page.

Please rely on original filings rather than on summaries. I ask only for a good-faith look and for ordinary due process. Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,
