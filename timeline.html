  19. AdvocacyAppeals / post-conviction

    Clemency first, conviction review second, new-evidence investigation third

    Date: 2026 — campaign priorities

    Priority 1 is a Virginia clemency petition: absolute pardon is the innocence track, officially a remedy of last resort, requiring exhaustion of judicial remedies including a Writ of Actual Innocence (no such writ has ever been filed in this case); conditional pardon is sentence modification and officially rare. Petitions go through the Secretary of the Commonwealth, the Parole Board investigates, there is no hearing and no appeal if denied, but refiling is allowed, and clemency power sits with Governor Abigail Spanberger. Priority 2 is the Arlington County Conviction Review Unit: an 11-page Screening Questionnaire to CWA_CRU@arlingtonva.us, for Arlington County or Falls Church felonies; claims already litigated before the original jury may be declined, review is never guaranteed, and questionnaire content can be used in court, so it should go through an attorney. Priority 3 is an independent investigation for genuinely new evidence, including a qualified request for post-conviction DNA testing of untested Items 5–11 and a location inquiry for sheet/hairs evidence whose preservation is unresolved — not a claim that evidence was destroyed, lost, or withheld.

    Source: Official Virginia clemency and Arlington CRU research, 14 October 2026
