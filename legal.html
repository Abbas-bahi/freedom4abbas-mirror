Privacy

The update form and the contact form do not send what you type to a server, and this site does not keep a mailing list. A provider can be connected later. Until then, do not use the forms for anything you need delivered.

Do not send sensitive legal evidence, confidential identities, or private contact details. No home address, private phone number, or private email is displayed, because none was provided for publication.

The preview host may use its own technical cookies to show the page. This site does not add an analytics tracker or an advertising pixel of its own.

Placeholders that need your confirmation

9 items are waiting. None of them should be read as case history.

  1. 1 · Why Review Is Needed

    What did the laboratory test, and what did it leave untested? The certificate reports no evidence of seminal fluid or blood on items 1 through 4, no spermatozoa on the listed smears, and no foreign DNA types on the penile swabs. It also reports no DNA result from the interior crotch of the underpants, and no analysis of items 5 through 11 after conversations with Assistant Commonwealth’s Attorney Molly Newton. A reviewer can ask what those choices mean. The certificate itself does not answer whether the charged acts occurred.

  2. 2 · Why Review Is Needed

    What do the defense psychology letters actually say about the interrogation? Marc J. Tabackman, Ph.D., wrote on April 15 and May 18, 2005 that he saw features he associates with a coerced-compliant statement, and that he could not say the confession was false. The May letter also records Ahmed’s report that he feared being beaten. That is an account in a defense letter, not proof of an assault. Defense counsel moved to suppress the statement; after a hearing on May 26, 2005, the court denied the motion on June 22, 2005. The motion papers do not state the grounds argued, and the hearing transcript has not been located.

  3. 3 · Why Review Is Needed

    What medical opinion was supplied, and what are its limits? Elisabeth Ridgely’s April 18, 2005 letter, summarized without the graphic detail, tells defense counsel that the findings she reviewed did not, in her opinion, conclusively show a sexual assault. An injury does not automatically prove the charged act, and the absence of an injury she considered significant does not automatically disprove it. She was retained as a defense consultant and did not testify at trial; Dr. Tabackman testified, limited to general risk factors. The images of her letter are withheld because they describe a child.

  4. 4 · Why Review Is Needed

    What review route is identified, and what is still missing? Arlington County’s Conviction Review Unit publicly accepts innocence claims and requests under the Virginia forensic-testing statute. The family record says ordinary appeals and habeas are exhausted and that an innocence writ has not been filed. The procedural history is documented from the court orders and dockets (see the Case page). Still not posted: the appellate opinions themselves, and a current chain of custody for the evidence in lab N04-7595.

  5. 5 · What supporters are asking for

    A conviction-review look at the record that exists. Advocacy request, not a finding. The family asks that the Arlington County Conviction Review Unit compare the laboratory certificate and the defense letters with the original trial record. The request does not ask anyone to treat this website as proof.

  6. 6 · What supporters are asking for

    A plain account of what was tested and where the evidence is. Advocacy request. The certificate shows that some items were not analyzed. The family record also describes a later preservation order that is not posted here; until it is produced, this site does not state that evidence was lost, destroyed, or hidden.

  7. 7 · What supporters are asking for

    Ordinary contact only. Advocacy limit. Letters should go to the public review office, not to private individuals, and should not name the complainant. The family’s belief in innocence can be stated as a belief.

  8. 8 · Take Action

    The outreach letter is addressed to the Arlington County Conviction Review Unit, using the public courthouse address and email checked on October 16, 2026. No application has been sent from this site. The sentencing orders are summarized on the Case page from the orders themselves. The appellate opinions and a current chain of custody for lab N04-7595 are still not posted.
