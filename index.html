The evidence and record supporting Abbas Ahmed’s innocence.

Abbas Ahmed is innocent.

This website presents the evidence, documents, and unanswered questions supporting Abbas Ahmed’s innocence and the campaign’s position that his conviction was wrongful.

Abbas has spent more than twenty years in a Virginia prison for a crime he did not commit, and he has never stopped saying so. He is not alone: the Mid-Atlantic Innocence Project reviewed his case and told him in writing, “We continue to believe in your innocence.” This site gathers the evidence, the documents, and the unanswered questions — and asks Virginia to look again.

What supporters are asking for

Three requests, plainly stated

These are the campaign's own requests, made in the family's name. Each one asks officials to look at the record that exists — nothing here asks anyone to take this website's word for anything.

  1. 01

    A conviction-review look at the record that exists

    Advocacy request, not a finding. The family asks that the Arlington County Conviction Review Unit compare the laboratory certificate and the defense letters with the original trial record. The request does not ask anyone to treat this website as proof.

  2. 02

    A plain account of what was tested and where the evidence is

    Advocacy request. The certificate shows that some items were not analyzed. The family record also describes a later preservation order that is not posted here; until it is produced, this site does not state that evidence was lost, destroyed, or hidden.

  3. 03

    Ordinary contact only

    Advocacy limit. Letters should go to the public review office, not to private individuals, and should not name the complainant. The family’s belief in innocence can be stated as a belief.
